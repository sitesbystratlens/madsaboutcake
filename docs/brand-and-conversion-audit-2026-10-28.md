# Mads About Cake — brand, landing-page and conversion review (28 October 2026)

## Design decision
Use logo pink **#F15EA0** for the December notice and graphic identity; logo peach **#FEDCDE** for the brand header/footer; deep plum **#553047** for main CTA backgrounds, key links and large headings; raspberry **#A53265** for small accent text with readable contrast; warm cream and warm-neutral for gallery and supporting sections.

Avoid evenly alternating pastel bands, repeated block components, saturated border separators, unverified popularity badges and large areas of logo pink. Real cakes should be the most colourful elements.

## Current site sections and customer journey
- Homepage: recognisable cake imagery, direct browse CTA, occasions by proof-grounded photo, craftsmanship and real session evidence, integrated grouped enquiry.
- Gallery: product, occasion and design-style filters; no verified bestseller badge.
- Decorate & Sip: more substantive description extracted from real posts, realistic session options, group enquiries, link to actual reel. The four-inch cake and three-colour buttercream palette were described by Mads in Instagram posts **DT3u87PkgOG** and **DF9zF8RhNRa**; a cupcake edition appears in **DHDXe4EB9Le**. Refreshments, travel and package details vary and remain subject to confirmation.
- About: use supporting brand story and actual creation photography without invented reviews.
- Forms: grouped enquiry categories; Formspree endpoint intentionally blank until authorised; do not claim a successful send.
- Footer: Sites by Stratlens links to **https://www.stratlens.com/websites/** everywhere.

## Caption-derived taxonomy
Of 233 posts authored by @madsaboutcake in the 27 October 2026 Apify file, removing hashtag boilerplate, keyword matches included: flavour/filling language in **184** posts, buttercream in **162**, sizing/tiers in **141**, floral terms in **37**, explicit cupcakes in **31**, and bows/ribbons in **24**. All are overlapping text matches, not distinct products or proof of demand.
Recommended structure: **Product (Cake, Cupcakes)** × **Occasion (Birthday, Wedding, Baby Celebration, Anniversary etc.)** × **Style (Vintage, Floral, Bow & Ribbon, Themed)**; flavour/size become detail attributes. Caption silence is not proof a style or occasion is absent.
 
## Owner confirmation before commercial launch
Photograph permissions, direct enquiry destination and tested Formspree flow, booking/package pricing, availability dates, location/travel arrangements, session inclusions, pickup/delivery and genuine testimonials. Keep the independent preview noindexed until approved.
