## Scope and interpretation
- **51 Keep**, **17 Remove**, **2 Undecided** from 70 distinct candidates (40 Birthday list; 34 Cupcake list; 4 overlap).
- **Keep** means include in *non-indexed concept preview*, **not** permission to publish owner images commercially.
- **Remove** means omit from the featured concept gallery. Existing historical catalogue records/source posts are not deleted.
- **Undecided** means keep the post in the review page without adding to the current concept gallery.
- Original featured 34 consisted of 6 birthdays, 6 weddings, 6 baby celebrations, 5 love and 11 signature/unspecified. The black-heart 30th birthday was removed from that featured set; **red-bow birthday remains**, by the owner's explicit Keep choice.
- Current concept gallery shows **74 unique designs**.
- Birthday occasion filter: **29** designs, including two Cupcake-list-only posts explicitly annotated 'birthday' by the owner.
- Cupcake product filter: **28** unique posts, including mixed cake/cupcake sets. A mixed set can appear under both Cakes and Cupcakes without duplicate gallery records.
- Weddings & engagements: **6** (previously selected six unchanged).
- Baby celebrations: **6** (previously selected six unchanged).
- Love & anniversaries: **5** (previously selected five unchanged, including the coconut cake and cupcake set).
- Signature/original unspecified: the original **11** unchanged; no assumption made about what the studio regards as a signature offering.

Two newly kept cupcake designs have occasion-related review notes but are **not yet added** to the six-item occasion filters: the Mayoun celebration cupcakes (pre-wedding) and an Oh Baby cupcake set (baby celebration). This honours the request not to change those established occasion groups while retaining the designs in the Cupcakes product filter.
