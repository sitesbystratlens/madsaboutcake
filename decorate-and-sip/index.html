PLANNING YOUR SESSION

A few things to know.

Can we book a private group?

Yes, we’ve hosted private hen’s parties and other group celebrations. Tell us your preferred date and group size, and we’ll check availability.

Where do sessions take place?

We’re based in Glen Iris, Melbourne, and can travel to other locations by arrangement. Let us know where you’d like to host your session.

What’s included in a session?

Our past sessions have included a four-inch cake to decorate, three buttercream colours, decorating tools and take-home packaging. Food, drinks and table settings may be arranged for some groups. We’ll confirm exactly what’s included for yours.

Can we decorate cupcakes instead?

Yes, we’ve run a cupcake edition too. Ask us if it’s available for your group.
