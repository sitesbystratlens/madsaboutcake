PLANNING YOUR SESSION

A few things to know.

Can I organise a private group?

Yes — Mads has shared private hen’s celebrations and other group sessions. Send your preferred date and group size to ask about availability.

Where are sessions held?

Mads has advertised sessions in Glen Iris, with travel available by arrangement. Include your preferred location when enquiring.

What’s included?

Published sessions have featured a four-inch decorating cake, a three-colour buttercream palette, tools and takeaway packaging. Grazing, beverages and themed table settings can be part of customised packages. Confirm your preferred inclusions with Mads.

Can we decorate cupcakes?

Mads has also hosted a cupcake edition of Decorate & Sip. Ask whether that format is available for your group.
