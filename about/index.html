A little about the studio

Mostly buttercream.

Mads About Cake makes custom cakes and cupcakes in Melbourne, with buttercream piping, bows, flowers, pearls and playful themes.

Her cakes start with different ideas: a colour, a celebration or a detailed brief. She also hosts Decorate & Sip group sessions, where guests decorate and take home their own cake.
