A little cake care guide

Cake pickup
& care.

Because we’ve put a lot of love into this beauty!

A few important tips to make sure your cake arrives safely and looks its best for the big moment.

🚗

01 / Transport

The journey home

  1. A safe spot for your cake

    Always keep your cake on a flat, stable surface during transport. If you’re driving alone, the passenger-side floor is usually your safest bet, or the boot if you can securely prevent the box from sliding around.

  2. If someone is holding it

    If your cake is travelling on someone’s lap, please hold the box firmly from the bottom. When going over bumps, speed bumps or making sudden stops, gently lift the box off your lap to minimise movement.

  3. Always support underneath

    Please avoid holding the box from the sides. Always support it from underneath, as squeezing the sides can push the box inwards and damage your cake. We don’t want any cake casualties!

🌡️

02 / The drive

Temperature matters

  • On warmer days, keep the car air-conditioned throughout the journey.
  • On colder days, try to minimise the heating or keep the cake away from direct heat sources.
  • Avoid leaving your cake in a parked car, especially in warm weather.
❄️

03 / At home

Once you’re home

  • Pop your cake straight into the fridge until you’re ready to serve.
  • For the best flavour and texture, take it out approximately 1–2 hours before serving (depending on the weather and room temperature).
💕

04 / One last thing

A final little reminder

I always recommend keeping your cake refrigerated until a couple of hours before your event. It’s the safest way to protect all those little details and prevent any unwanted melting, sliding or decorating disasters before the celebrations even begin!

Thank you for trusting me with your special occasion. Now go enjoy the celebrations – and please treat your cake kindly!
